It concedes what should be conceded, inventories what the receipts actually bought against the real goal, and charters the phase that aims at it.

## 1. The concession, stated plainly

The program's true goal was always: make a modified 0.5B Qwen smarter — better answers, judged against the 14B teacher — using recurrence, a scratchpad, and speculative decoding. After the Stage A negative, the success currency was changed to speculative acceptance because it was measurable and reachable. That was the right de-risking move at the time. But every gate written since then encodes *non-inferiority* as the quality target — "keep 99.5% of the baseline's decisions" — and a program whose quality ceiling is "don't get worse" cannot, by construction, produce "smarter." The gates did exactly what they were designed to do, and what they were designed to do was the wrong ambition. The preservation losses, the 2%-open bridge gate, the tiny writeback band (1–5% of hidden-state RMS): the system was *trained not to change the answers*, and then measured as not changing the answers. That is the trap, and it was self-built.

## 2. What the receipts actually bought — read against the real goal

The campaign looks different when inventoried as assets for answer improvement rather than as a speed result:

1. **The state works.** The flow module moves the scratchpad 1.36 nats toward the 14B teacher's future distribution — thirteen times its gate. There is now a trained component, inside the 0.5B's forward pass, that demonstrably contains information about what the 14B would do next. That was science fiction at Stage A.
2. **The channel works and its ceiling is an intelligence ceiling.** This is the number that says the goal is alive: oracle-aimed writes through the bounded bridge flip **60.85% of the positions where the student is wrong and the teacher is right** (at the measured safe radius), with near-zero collateral. The phase-1 oracle ceilings on *final answers* were **+3.91 to +5.61 points** — real intelligence gains, sitting in the channel we built, reachable if and only if the write is aimed. The gap between our +0.5%-acceptance result and those ceilings is not architecture. It is aim.
3. **Aim has never been trained.** Read that again, because it is the pivot. The V-series proved aimed writes work. The training campaign then supervised the bridge with *acceptance* losses and *preservation* losses — never once with the aim signal itself. The oracle first-order direction (which write would flip this position to the teacher's token) was computed, validated, and then used only as a curriculum weight, because it was barred from *inference*. But it was never barred from *training* — supervising the bridge to produce the oracle's write at training time is exactly how EAGLE trains its head on teacher features, and it is deployable-legal because inference uses only the trained bridge. The strongest signal the program ever measured (98.4% flip rate when aimed) has been sitting unused as a training target.
4. **The curriculum instrument already exists.** The teacher lattice carries teachability T(K), cross-scale agreement, and verifier labels for 190,000 anchors — built two months ago for exactly this and never yet used to train answers. The high-teachability stratum (teacher right, student wrong, gap learnable) is the answer-improvement training set, already collected, already hashed.
5. **The optimization craft is solved.** Staged curricula, directional gradient contracts, calibration-derived weights, the guardrail doctrine. The next campaign starts where this one ended: knowing how to train these hybrids without the machinery fighting itself. That knowledge cost four false stops and a failed pilot, and it transfers whole.

## 3. Phase 3: the Answer-Gap Program

**The objective flips.** Primary training signal: teacher-alignment of the *final prediction* — KL from the sidecar-augmented student to the 14B teacher, and cross-entropy on teacher-verified answers, concentrated on the high-teachability stratum. The drafter and its acceptance stay as a secondary output (a model whose next-token distribution is closer to the teacher accepts longer drafts for free — acceptance becomes the byproduct it always should have been).

**The currency flips.** The reported number is **gap closed**: (augmented − base) / (14B − base) on task batteries — the Paper One evaluation infrastructure (ARC battery, Tier-1 style checks) extended with reasoning and code sets. Zero means the current result. One means a 0.5B that answers like a 14B. The program stops grading itself on a currency where "neutral" can look like success.

**Aim gets supervised.** The new core loss: the bridge's write is trained against the oracle first-order direction at training time — direction distillation, the measured 98.4%-when-aimed signal finally used as a target. The first experiment of the phase is the cheap falsifier: an aimed-writeback training pilot on DEV, measuring what fraction of the oracle flip rate a *trained* aim captures, and at what collateral. If trained aim captures a substantial fraction of oracle aim, the +3.91-to-+5.61-point ceilings become a roadmap. If it captures little, that is the honest boundary of the approach and it will have cost one exploration run to find.

**Preservation anneals instead of walling.** Stage A justified fear of unaimed writes. Supervised aim is the thing that was never tried, and the safety machinery should scale with demonstrated competence: preservation pressure starts where it is, and *relaxes on an explicit schedule tied to measured aim precision* — as the bridge demonstrates it flips wrong answers without breaking right ones, the gates are allowed open wider, the writeback band rises, the preserve-KL weight falls. The floor that never moves is the honest one: a catastrophe bound on a reference battery (the doctrine's fourth cliff), not a change-nothing contract. Gates become schedules with revisit clauses. Thresholds that exist to protect exploration never again get to define its ambition.

**Loops become reasoning.** With an answer objective, the K ≤ 4 recurrent refinements are iterative reasoning steps, and answer accuracy as a function of K is finally the measurement the program was founded to make. If the state improves with iteration and the aimed bridge converts state into answers, depth buys correctness — the original thesis, now testable on working machinery.

**Method commitments against the trap, per the review.** Phase 3 opens as *exploration* under the guardrail doctrine: cliffs keep hard stops, everything else measures, and no confirmation gate is written until the mechanism shows life. Every pre-specified threshold in the phase carries either an annealing schedule or a named revisit condition. And a short research sweep precedes the design lock — future-token and hidden-state distillation, self-correction training, latent lookahead — because the review's last instruction is the right one: work around traps with research and creativity, not only with receipts.

## 4. What happens to Paper Two (revised per Mark's ruling, 2026-08-09)

**Everything banked so far is the methods portion of Paper Two. The results section is Phase 3's outcome: the recurrent model trained to outperform base Qwen 0.5B, measured relative to Qwen 14B.** The paper does not ship on the acceptance result — it waits for the answer-gap result and is structured around it. Concretely: the current draft v2 becomes the methods skeleton — architecture, the Phase 1 negatives and measured physics, the training governance, the staged curriculum, and the acceptance confirmation, which is retained as a *methods-validation* result (the channel is causal, trainable, and quality-bounded, demonstrated end-to-end under preregistration) rather than as the headline. The headline claim the paper is written to carry is the gap-closed number: how much of the distance from base 0.5B to 14B the trained recurrent system recovers, on task batteries, with the oracle ceilings (+3.91 to +5.61 points) as the measured room and the aimed-writeback training as the mechanism that pursues it. The honest bridge sentence between the two halves: *the system of the methods sections was trained under a preservation contract that bounds it near neutrality, and the measured oracle ceilings show the same channel carries several points of unrealized answer improvement — the results sections report what happens when the objective is flipped to claim it.* If Phase 3 succeeds, Paper Two is the paper the program set out to write. If Phase 3 finds a boundary instead, the paper reports that boundary with the same receipts discipline, and the methods half stands unchanged either way.

## 5. The first three moves

1. **Research sweep** (strategy, no GPU): latent/feature distillation for answer improvement, direction-supervised interventions, self-correction literature — one memo, before any design lock.
2. **Aimed-writeback pilot charter** (strategy → coding): train the bridge against oracle directions on the high-teachability stratum, DEV-only, exploration rules, one A100 session. The single question: what fraction of oracle aim does trained aim capture? Every later decision keys off that fraction.
3. **Gap-closed benchmark assembly** (coding, CPU-heavy): the battery, the 14B reference scores, the gap arithmetic — so the phase's currency exists before its first training run, and no one ever again discovers at confirmation time that the program has been measuring the wrong thing.

## 6. Plain-language summary, because this memo is the plain language

The program set out to make a small model smarter and ended up proving it could make one imperceptibly faster without getting dumber — because after an early disaster, every rule we wrote was a rule against getting dumber, and rules against getting dumber cannot make anything smarter. But the wreckage bought the three things the real goal needs: a latent state that genuinely knows what the big teacher would think, a safe channel into the small model's computation whose measured ceiling is several points of real answer improvement, and the training craft to teach these parts without them strangling each other. The one thing never tried is the obvious thing: teaching the channel to aim, using the aiming signal we validated months ago and then filed away. Phase 3 flips the objective to answers, grades itself on the fraction of the 14B gap it closes, supervises the aim directly, and lets the safety rails loosen as competence is demonstrated instead of standing forever at the height of our worst day. The first experiment is cheap and decisive either way. And the paper is no longer two papers or a foundation-plus-sequel: the work so far is the methods half, training the recurrent bridge sidecar to close the gap is the results half, and together they are Paper Two.
