## 2. The three questions

**Question 1 — floor receipt: satisfied as scoped, and the scope correction is accepted.** The 22.7–23.4 percent floored fraction is a whitening-spectrum statement — roughly 98–99 of 128 per-slot directions carry statistically supported variance, regularization is materially active, the relative floor binds, the absolute epsilon never engages, condition number exactly 1/tau. That satisfies the owed receipt. The r_c = 256 rank question is a different object, correctly distinguished: **the nonblocking cached singular-spectrum table for the RRR map is authorized** (CPU, cached, private records), because the paper will want a direct rank-support statement and because a steeply decaying RRR spectrum would inform a v2 rank reduction. Nonblocking means exactly that — it does not gate the pilots.

**Question 2 — the 0.86 initial trust ratio: a preregistration amendment is adopted now, before lock, rather than telemetry-only.** The build receipt exposed a denominator artifact, not a safety signal: the anchor-dominated initial state has RMS ≈ 0.02 by design, so a state-referenced ratio is inflated at init by construction, and with L_trust active from Phase A the rail fires from step zero — pressing the magnitude head *down* precisely when the direction network has learned nothing. A rail that punishes the initialization the spec itself mandates is measuring the wrong quantity. Amendment, binding in the locked protocol and as a one-line build change with its assertion updated: the trust ratio becomes **r_k = RMS(Δz_k) / (max(RMS(z_k), RMS(sg[Z_T])) + eps)** — endpoint-referenced. The endpoint is the natural unit of the flow (every target is a fraction of remaining displacement toward Z_T), so under the amendment r_0 ≈ 0.018 at init (rail silent), the rail binds only when updates grow large relative to where the flow is going (the actual runaway condition), and late in the flow, where RMS(z_k) ≈ RMS(Z_T), the amended and original definitions coincide. r_max = 0.5 and weight 0.01 are unchanged. **Both ratios are logged from step zero** — the state-referenced ratio remains mandatory telemetry (it is the settling diagnostic the coding agent flagged) — along with rent contribution, clip fractions, and gate-open rate. **Abort boundary, preregistered**: if the amended trust penalty is nonzero on more than 50 percent of steps in any 100-step window after warmup, the arm halts and reports rather than continuing to burn budget; that is persistent saturation, which under the amended denominator can only mean genuine runaway.

**Question 3 — pilot constants: accepted with four amendments, no mechanism-installation receipt needed.** AdamW, lr 3e-4, weight decay 0.01, batch 128, 1,000 steps, seeds {0, 1} are reasonable for a 1.2M-parameter adapter pilot. Amendments, all binding: (i) **weight decay excludes biases, RMSNorm gains, and all learnable scalars** (gate biases, magnitude-head bias, g_k, rho_k, b_{k,j}) — this one is load-bearing, because decay on the −4.0 magnitude and gate biases would pull them toward zero and *open the gates by optimizer side-effect*, silently defeating the closed-at-init design; (ii) linear warmup 100 steps; (iii) evaluation cadence every 100 steps on a fixed matched DEV eval slice, identical rows across arms and seeds; (iv) fp32 on every recurrent-gradient path (RG-11) and the spec §7 per-module clip constants, restated in the protocol so the launcher asserts them.

## 4. Adequacy precondition on the equivalence verdict

An under-trained pilot makes all arms look equivalent and hands the default a spurious win. Before the equivalence band may be invoked, the pooled runs must show minimal progress: flow validation loss improved by at least 20 percent from its step-100 value, and mean gate-open rate risen above its initialization value, in at least four of the six runs. If the precondition fails, the budget doubles once (to 2,000 steps, same seeds, resumed not restarted) and the rule is then applied as written. One extension, preregistered, no further budget growth without a new strategy decision. If the precondition fails again at 2,000 steps, that is itself the pilot result — the Phase-A objective does not move this module at this scale — and it returns to strategy, not to more steps.

## 7. Plain-language summary

The tie-breaker experiment gave a clean answer: the small advantage was real, not luck — three matched re-runs reproduced it and the luck component measured four times smaller than the effect. So the state-compression method is chosen and frozen. The full controller is also built and provably inert until trained: with it switched off, the base model's outputs are bit-for-bit unchanged. Two details surfaced and both are handled: about a quarter of the compressed directions carry so little signal that the safety floor props them up (fine for now, and we've asked for one cheap table that would tell us whether the space should shrink in version two), and the brand-new controller's first nudge looks huge relative to its deliberately tiny starting state — so the safety brake, which would have engaged on day one for no good reason, now measures nudges against the destination instead of the starting point, and we log both views. The training test plan is now locked: three whitening settings, two seeds each, fixed optimizer with one subtle fix (the optimizer's weight-shrinking feature must not touch the gate settings, or it would slowly pry open gates we designed to start closed), a written rule for picking the winner, a tie-goes-to-the-default band, and a progress check that prevents an undercooked test from handing the default a hollow victory. One loose end: the V1d safety report is still owed before the final training phase can lock.
