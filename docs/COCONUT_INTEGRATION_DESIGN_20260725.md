# Composite Integration Design — Coconut-Style Horizontal Looping on the Recurrent Qwen Surgery

Draft 1, 2026-07-25. Strategy lane. Design and test specification for Mark's markup, then coding-agent implementation. Nothing here authorizes training — this is the engineering substrate for C1, buildable and testable now on the Qwen2.5-0.5B surgery where the existing assertions live, then ported to the re-cut Qwen3-0.6B after the S2 preflight. Note (2026-07-25): the gate-token strategy for the Qwen3 port — whether the latent segment reuses the native think pair or gets its own markers — is an open strategy question under review; see the concept note, section 5.3. It does not affect the Qwen2.5 battery work, which uses the reserved control trio throughout.

Reference implementation: facebookresearch/coconut (coconut.py). Its load-bearing mechanics, confirmed 2026-07-25: the forward runs max-latents-plus-one passes, each computing up to the next latent position. The latent token's embedding is replaced by the *preceding position's* final-layer hidden state, with the replacement done by decomposing inputs_embeds into lists and reconstructing — never in place. The KV cache is sliced to the processed prefix and reused across passes without detach, so gradients flow through cached keys and values. Loss masking lives in the labels. There is no detach anywhere in the training path — the entire chain is one graph.

## 1. Why gradient nullification is the central risk

The horizontal chain's entire training signal travels one route: answer-token loss → back through the final pass → into the fed-back hidden states → through every earlier latent step's full stack → to the prompt. Any detach, any no_grad wrapper, any cache stored outside the graph, any in-place mutation of inputs_embeds silently cuts that route, and the model still trains — the answer head learns to ignore the latent slots and loss still falls. That is the signature failure: everything looks alive while the mechanism under test is dead. This program has met it before: Paper One's bridge gradient flow had to be repaired, and Arm G's integrity checks exist because live-looking training is not evidence of a live pathway. The battery in section 5 is designed so that a dead chain cannot show green.

## 2. Proposed surgical modifications (both budgets)

**M1 — Horizontal feedback path.** Coconut-standard: the final post-norm hidden state at the position preceding a latent slot becomes the inputs_embeds at that slot. In our architecture the fed state therefore re-enters through the full stack — prelude, recurrent block times L, coda — at the latent position. This is the native-interface route (input embedding space), deliberately unlike Arm G's failed residual injection.

**M2 — Horizontal bridge, identity-initialized.** A linear map W = I + ΔW with ΔW zero-initialized between the fed hidden state and the embedding slot. At step zero this is exactly Coconut's raw feedback, so the identity contract is testable, and it becomes trainable if the final-hidden distribution and the embedding distribution disagree in scale or geometry on this substrate (Coconut's identity worked on GPT-2; Qwen2.5's embedding scale is not guaranteed to match its post-norm hidden scale). Preflight diagnostic: compare RMS statistics of final hidden states against embedding rows before deciding whether ΔW stays or an RMS-matching scale is added. This mirrors the vertical re-entry bridge lesson — the interface between representation spaces is where our surgery has needed repair before.

**M3 — Latent placeholder token.** Reuse `<|recur_readout|>` as the latent token id — its T0 registration as an internal hidden-state anchor is exactly this role. Its embedding row is replaced at every latent slot, so the row itself must receive no gradient from those positions (tested). `<|recur_continue|>` and `<|recur_stop|>` stay reserved for C2's horizontal gate and are unused in C1 integration, where H is fixed.

**M4 — Two-dimensional execution bookkeeping.** The T0 loop-accounting contract extends to a grid: requested and executed counts per (pass, vertical loop), recorded and asserted under forcing. Counter convention (corrected 2026-07-25 during implementation): H is the number of latent slots, and Coconut performs H plus 1 passes — H feedback-producing plus the final answer pass — so total block applications are (H plus 1) times L, of which H times L produce fed-back states. Both counters are recorded and asserted: the total catches a skipped or doubled pass, the feedback count catches a latent slot whose state never re-entered. Vertical loops per latent step are fixed per experiment (L of 1 in the first pilot, per section 7).

**M5 — Multi-pass forward, recompute-first (amended 2026-07-25 per the RG-0 audit).** Adopt Coconut's segmentation: compute to the next latent position, replace the latent embedding by list-reconstruction, position ids continuous through latent slots, attention including them, the visible-generation mask for the three control tokens in force. The RG-0 audit found the training path graph-live with use_cache False, cache disabled under gradient checkpointing, and cache rejected at L above 1. The first implementation is therefore the full recompute graph — mechanically safe by construction. Cache reuse exists only inside the RG-5 equivalence test at L of 1, where the composite layer explicitly allocates and crops a DynamicCache. Training at L above 1 is recompute-only.

**M6 — Budget variants.** Full-block: recurrent block, vertical bridge, horizontal bridge, and control rows train. Adapter: R16 adapters, both bridges, and control rows train with the backbone frozen. The adapter budget carries the sharpest silent-kill hazard: frozen must mean requires_grad False, never a no_grad wrapper or an eval-mode shortcut, because gradients must still flow *through* the frozen backbone to reach earlier latent steps.

## 3. Hazard inventory (what the tests are aimed at)

1. Detach or no_grad anywhere in the multi-pass path, including inside cache handling, EMA hooks, or logging code that touches live tensors.
2. Cache tensors stored outside the graph (cloned-detached past_key_values), truncating backpropagation to earlier passes while later-pass gradients look healthy.
3. In-place mutation of inputs_embeds or residual buffers breaking the graph with no error (or raising only under detect_anomaly).
4. Frozen-backbone wrappers blocking flow-through in the adapter budget.
5. The readout embedding row leaking gradient from replaced positions, or optimizer/EMA groups not matching the intended trainable set.
6. Autocast/bf16 at the feedback boundary producing NaN or silent underflow across H stacked passes.
7. Gradient checkpointing interacting with cache reuse (HF disables cache under checkpointing — silently, in some versions).
8. Vanishing gradient across the chain: at H steps and L loops the graph is H times (12 plus 12L) layers deep — not a bug, but a measured profile with a red condition at exact zero.
9. Position-id or attention-mask errors that exclude latent slots, making them unreadable downstream (the chain then trains but carries nothing).

File the inventory as a receipt. ## 5. Red/green battery

Each test states its exact pass condition. Red on any failure. RG-1 through RG-11 are plumbing (pre-training, small fixed batch). RG-12 is the post-pilot proof that the mechanism is used, not just differentiable.

- **RG-1 Composite-off identity.** H equals 0 → output logits within 1e-3 of the registered surgery, both budgets, at L of 1 and at forced L. The T0 identity contract, extended.
- **RG-2 Step-zero bridge identity.** With ΔW at zero, the composite forward equals raw Coconut feedback exactly (allclose, fp32).
- **RG-3 Chain reachability (amended 2026-07-25).** Scalar loss at the final answer position: autograd.grad is finite and nonzero with respect to (a) the fed embedding at latent step 1 and (b) the prompt embedding activations. Implementation finding: when every embedding row is frozen (the adapter budget), PyTorch builds no graph from the embedding output, so probe (b) comes back empty for instrumentation reasons, not because the chain is dead. The composite therefore inserts an audit-only leaf activation at the embedding boundary when no upstream embedding parameter is trainable — the table is neither unfrozen nor updated. Hardening: the leaf is gated behind an explicit audit flag, training mode asserts the flag off, and RG-1's identity contract runs in both flag states to prove the probe inert.
- **RG-4 Finite-difference agreement (procedure revised 2026-07-25 after the first battery run).** Perturb the fed hidden state at step j by epsilon times a fixed direction: the observed loss change matches the analytic directional derivative under the original 10 percent relative criterion. Catches gradients that are nonzero but wrong. First-run finding: a single fixed epsilon missed tolerance by 5.43e-4 while every direction-sensitive check (RG-5 gradient cosine 1.0, RG-10 exact) passed — a single epsilon cannot distinguish a bad derivative from fp32 cancellation. Revised procedure: an fp32 epsilon-stability sweep at fixed direction and fixed weights, passing when at least two adjacent epsilon values agree with the analytic derivative under the original criterion. The threshold is unchanged — the measurement improved.
- **RG-5 Cache equivalence, rescoped 2026-07-25.** At L of 1 only: sliced-DynamicCache multi-pass versus full single-graph recompute — identical logits and identical gradients on probe parameters (fp32 reference on 128-token sequences, stated atol). Catches truncated-cache backprop. Precondition, added after the first defect (below): before the equivalence comparison runs, assert the cache is actually populated — non-empty, expected layer count, expected prefix lengths — because an unpopulated cache silently recomputes and makes equivalence vacuously green. At L above 1 the test converts to a guard assertion: the cache path must refuse to engage, so training there is provably recompute-only. Rerun in the checkpointing configuration actually used for training. The Transformers version is pinned in the RG-5 receipt, since the fix below selects keywords by signature and could silently no-op again on a future version.

  Defect log, 2026-07-25: RG-5 exposed a version-compatibility defect in the wrapper's nominal cache path — the older singular past_key_value keyword against current Transformers' past_key_values, so the cache never populated. Impact statement: every registered run used use_cache False, so no registered receipt touched the defective path and nothing requires reconciliation. Fix: signature-based keyword selection, path held red until logits and gradients match recomputation with the populated-cache precondition passing.

  Resolution, 2026-07-25 (first battery run): with the cache populated, the sliced path preserved gradient direction exactly (cosine 1.0) but missed strict logit and absolute-gradient equivalence (max differences 8.51e-5 and 2.86e-4). **Sliced cache is retired from the authorized training path permanently.** Full recomputation is the sole training path at every L. RG-5's residual role: the L-above-1 refusal guard stays, and the equivalence receipts stand as documentation of a rejected optimization.
- **RG-6 Frozen-but-transparent (adapter budget).** Backbone parameter gradients exactly zero or None, while activation gradients at earlier latent steps are nonzero. Frozen weights, transparent graph.
- **RG-7 Application accounting (counters corrected and instrumentation revised 2026-07-25).** Forward and backward counters on the recurrent block equal (H plus 1) times L exactly for total applications and H times L for feedback-producing applications, per the M4 convention, and the per-(pass, loop) gradient-norm map has no exact-zero entries. Instrumentation finding: module-level full_backward_hook fires ambiguously when a weight-tied module is reused in a branched graph, so backward counting uses tensor-level hooks on each recorded application's output — one unambiguous callback per grid cell. Hook closures record norms, never retained tensors, so the counter does not hold the graph in memory across the grid. Extends the house liveness-assertion pattern to the grid.
- **RG-8 Row-leak check.** Gradient on the `<|recur_readout|>` embedding row from replaced positions is exactly zero, and optimizer plus EMA groups cover exactly the intended trainable set (hash the parameter-name sets).
- **RG-9 Anomaly pass.** One forward-backward under torch.autograd.set_detect_anomaly(True) with no error — catches in-place graph breaks and version-counter violations.
- **RG-10 Checkpointing equivalence.** Gradients with and without activation checkpointing agree within tolerance in the exact training configuration.
- **RG-11 Precision boundary (policy comparison ordered 2026-07-25 after the first battery run).** Per-example fed-gradient cosine against the fp32 reference above 0.99, NaN/Inf guards at the feedback boundary across all H passes. First-run finding: full bf16 measured 0.983584 — below threshold, all states finite. The threshold does not move. Ordered follow-up: compare full fp32, full bf16, and fp32 master weights under bf16 autocast on a small fixed prompt set, judged on graph numerics only, never on answer accuracy. Added diagnostic: measure the cosine as a function of H (2, 4, 8) per policy — the fed-gradient error should compound across horizontal passes just as the EMA audit showed parameter perturbation compounding across loops, and C1 runs larger H than the preflight. One declared production policy must pass at the H range C1 will use.
- **RG-12 authorization condition (set 2026-07-25).** RG-12's pilot is authorized only when the recompute path passes the revised RG-4 epsilon-stability diagnostic and one declared production precision policy passes RG-11 at the C1 H range. No threshold from the first battery run is relaxed. First-run status: 8 of 11 contracts passed — connectivity, identity, accounting, transparency, and checkpointing are all established; the three misses are verification-numerics items covered by the two follow-ups above.
- **RG-12 Causal-use probe (post-pilot).** After a short uncitable pilot on an existing synthetic family: replacing the fed hidden state at step j with zeros or a shuffled batch-mate's state must shift the answer distribution by more than a stated KL floor, and the gradient-norm-by-step profile must have no exact-zero steps. A model that passes RG-1 through RG-11 but fails RG-12 has learned to ignore its latent chain — plumbing green, mechanism dead, and only this test sees it.

## 6. Sequencing

Build and green RG-0 through RG-11 on the Qwen2.5-0.5B surgery now-ish — it is engineering, not registered science, it touches no registered artifact, and it can proceed in parallel with T1-lite since it trains nothing. RG-12's pilot waits for the T1-lite verdict like everything else with a training loop. Port to Qwen3-0.6B after the S2 re-cut preflight, rerunning the full battery there — the battery is substrate-agnostic by construction. C1's compression curriculum then starts from a mechanically verified composite rather than debugging gradients and curriculum simultaneously, which is exactly the co-debugging trap the gate-order discipline exists to prevent.

## 8. M7 — per-position depth-by-append with transient eviction (added 2026-07-28)

Added after the D0 causal allocation audit exposed the harm asymmetry of in-place vertical looping (30,008 hurts against 8,564 helps at the 1→2 transition) and Mark elected to merge the depth axis into the horizontal mechanism. Rationale in COMPOSITE_LATENT_LOOPING_CONCEPT_20260723.md section 5.5; experimental order in STRATEGY_TO_CODING_AGENT_COMPOSITE_DEPTH_BY_APPEND_20260728.md (Drive `17rrJ14UAk--FnVwRVliP1NDM7MF2HOBA`, SHA `4a70c7fca81d24a4b0c36bc085695bec54eade3f0bb5801d3971b04c4a0d99f8`).

**Mechanism.** At a decision position t, extra computation is spent by appending k latent positions after t rather than re-applying the recurrent block to t in place. Each appended slot uses the `<|recur_readout|>` id per M3, its embedding replaced by the final post-norm hidden state of the preceding position per M1, passed through the horizontal bridge held at identity per M2 and the section 7 resolution. Position ids run continuous through the appended slots, attention includes them, and the visible-generation mask on the control trio stays in force. The next real token is read at the last appended slot. Position t's state is never overwritten and stays in the cache throughout — the non-destructive property is the whole content of the modification.

**Difference from C1's H.** In C1, H is a fixed count of latent slots preceding an answer. In M7, k is a per-position quantity that varies with the token being decided, and the appended slots sit between two real tokens of an ongoing sequence. The counter convention is unchanged in form: a decision at k appended slots costs (k plus 1) times L block applications, of which k times L produce fed-back states.

**Eviction.** The k appended slots are dropped from the KV cache once the token is emitted, and subsequent real tokens carry the position ids of the unmodified sequence. This keeps the visible sequence exactly aligned with the plain drafter's, so the k of 0 path is bit-comparable to the baseline and the helps/hurts decomposition compares like with like. The persistent-scratchpad variant — slots retained for all later tokens — is the more interesting long-run design and opens an additive cross-token channel that would need its own characterization. It is out of scope for DC0 and pinned.

**Contracts.** M4's counters extend to the append grid with a third counter for evicted slots per decision position, asserted under forcing: an eviction that silently fails converts the experiment into the persistent variant undetected. RG-1 extends to k of 0 identity within 1e-3 at L of 1 and at forced L, both budgets. The M2 RMS diagnostic — final post-norm hidden state statistics against embedding rows — is promoted from a preflight nicety to a precondition, because a scale mismatch would make an untrained append look catastrophic for reasons unrelated to the geometry under test.

**Gradient status.** DC0, the first M7 experiment, is forward-only and takes no gradients, so the RG-4 epsilon-stability sweep and the RG-11 precision comparison are not preconditions for it. They remain preconditions for any M7 configuration with a training loop, including the bridge-adaptation contingency held for markup in the handoff's section 3.4.
