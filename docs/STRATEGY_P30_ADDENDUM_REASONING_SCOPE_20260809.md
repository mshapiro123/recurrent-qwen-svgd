# Strategy — P3.0 Addendum: Reasoning, Not Knowledge, and the Cascade Already Knew

Date: 2026-08-09. Origin: Mark's two rulings on the P3.0 sweep. Amends the P3.0 memo (Drive `1p1Otx5wPNQA1d5Eg07OR9RX6F3kIZ4Mb`) and binds the P3.1–P3.4 locks. Both rulings are adopted.

## 1. The same-family caution was already answered by our own design — the risk register overstated it

The P3.0 sweep logged the 2026 on-policy-distillation finding — same-family models of different sizes converge to similar distributions, so raw size gap does not guarantee transferable signal — as an external caution. Mark's correction: **the training selection was built on cross-scale agreement across several Qwen 2.5 family sizes from the start.** The lattice's banked quantities include cross-scale agreement precisely so that the program never trains on "the 14B said so" alone. Positions enter the teachability stratum because the larger models agree with each other while the student diverges — which is the definition of a scale-consistent signal rather than a single teacher's idiosyncrasy. The literature's caution applies to programs that distill blindly from one big sibling. Ours measured the disagreement structure across the family (the 16.748% cascade rate is a banked receipt) before asking anyone to learn from it. The risk register entry is rewritten accordingly: the caution is *answered by an existing instrument*, and the lock-level binding is that the P3.2 stratum definition **requires cross-scale agreement** (14B and the 32B cascade tier concurring where the lattice provides both) as a stratum criterion, not merely a recorded field.

## 2. Scope ruling: Phase 3 measures and instills reasoning, not knowledge

**The ruling.** At 0.5B scale the program is trying to instill *reasoning* — the ability to compute its way to answers the base model fails to reach — not to inject stored facts. Knowledge injection is a different mechanism with a different architecture, and it is **Paper Three: the knowledge sidecar.** Phase 3 does not chase knowledge gaps, does not claim knowledge gains, and does not interpret a knowledge-gap failure as a reasoning-boundary result.

This ruling resolves a tension the sweep surfaced without naming: the Small Model Learnability Gap's "missing domain knowledge" mechanism — a leading reason small students fail to absorb large-teacher signal — describes exactly the component of the 14B−base gap this phase now explicitly does not pursue. The gap has a reasoning component (the student has the facts but cannot compute the path) and a knowledge component (the student never stored the fact, and no amount of recurrence will conjure it). Recurrent depth, a scratchpad, and an aimed write channel are reasoning machinery. Aiming them at knowledge gaps would be spending the wrong instrument on the wrong deficit and then reading the miss as a boundary of the instrument.

**Bindings on the locks:**

- **Battery roles (P3.1 lock).** Target batteries — the ones gap_closed is claimed on — are the *verifiable-reasoning* sets: the math battery (GSM8K) and the code battery (MBPP) as primary, ARC-Challenge as secondary (retained for Paper One continuity, mixed reasoning/knowledge character noted). The MMLU slice and the Tier-1 set are the **floor and retention battery only**: the program defends them (the −3 cliff, the A1/B4 non-negative pooled accounting) and never targets them. ARC-Easy moves to the floor group with them. The claim-coherence arithmetic of t1 is unchanged — knowledge batteries still sit inside the pooled non-negativity constraint — but no knowledge battery appears in the numerator of a headline claim.
- **Stratum filter (P3.2 lock).** The oracle cache's stratum takes a reasoning weighting: cross-scale-consistent teachable positions (§1), drawn preferentially from the verified stratum's reasoning domains (math and code training splits, where correctness is computed, not recalled) and from lattice positions whose teachability profile indicates a computable gap. The lock states the filter's fields and the fraction of the cache drawn from each source.
- **The mix-teacher lever, reframed (P3.5 queue).** The Qwen2.5-3B pass is not only learnability medicine — it is a **reasoning-band instrument**. Positions a 3B solves that the 0.5B misses are, with high probability, within-reach-by-computation rather than gated on stored knowledge the whole small family lacks. Positions only the 14B-and-up tier reaches are disproportionately knowledge-gated — Paper Three's territory, catalogued now, pursued later. If the lever fires, its lattice pass doubles as the program's first empirical knowledge/reasoning decomposition of the gap.
- **Claim language (paper ledger).** The confirmed claim is denominated in reasoning-battery gap closed. The bounded phrasing for the manuscript: the system closes a measured fraction of the base-to-14B gap *on verifiable reasoning tasks*, with knowledge-dominated performance defended but not claimed. The Paper Two → Paper Three arc is stated in the program-structure paragraph of the introduction, as Paper One's was: recurrence for reasoning first, the knowledge sidecar as the registered successor.

## 3. What this changes in the published P3.0 memo

Section 2's risk-register entry (same-family caution) is superseded by §1 above. Section 5's battery references inherit the target/floor role split of §2. The P3.5 lever queue's mix-teacher entry inherits the reframing. The sweep's substantive findings — the LOTUS slot-CE arm, the SCoRe loop-collapse telemetry, the on-policy lever, the forward-KL v1 justification — are unaffected: all of them are reasoning-machinery findings, which is consistent with the ruling rather than coincidental, because the sweep was scoped to the charter's four streams and the charter was always aimed at reasoning. The ruling makes the aim explicit where the documents had left it implied, and Paper Three now has its first sentence on the record.
