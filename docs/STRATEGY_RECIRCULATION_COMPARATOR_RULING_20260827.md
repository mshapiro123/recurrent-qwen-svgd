## 0. Plain-language summary

The probe's first gate did its job. Before running any recirculation cells, the coding agent
re-scored the untouched model on our 461-question battery using the probe's own evaluator —
the one every recirculation cell will use — with recirculation switched fully off (α=0). The
identity check was perfect: with recirculation off, the new evaluator produces bit-for-bit
identical logits and cache to a plain forward pass. But the score came out 160/461 instead of
the 162/461 we banked from the earlier depth study. That is not a bug and not a real change in
the model: the two evaluators schedule their arithmetic differently, and in BF16 that reshuffles
rounding enough to flip a handful of borderline questions each way (15 lost, 13 gained, 147
shared — net −2, well inside our established ±6–9 cross-runtime noise floor, with the same
signature we've measured before: long math generations flip, short factual questions don't).
The rule we've enforced all program: comparisons are only valid between runs that share an
evaluator. So the recirculation experiment's baseline is 160, measured by its own evaluator —
not 162 imported from a different one. The success bars were always defined relative to
baseline (+20 to claim a real effect, −9 as the edge of harmless), so they now read 180 and
151. Nothing is scrubbed: 162 stays banked as the depth study's number under its evaluator.
The agent now resumes the remaining Phase-0 steps (timing, the Gemma sanity anchor, and the
cost projection) before any real cells run.

## 1. R-A — RATIFIED: 160/461 is the paper-native α=0 comparator

The row receipt (1,748,126 bytes, SHA-256
`79fb3b1a28780b24b2a8db0a99f701f8ab86af707086de778f975696e072e41e`) generated by the
paper-native token-sequential evaluator at α=0 is ratified as the recirculation probe's
baseline. All Phase-A cell classifications (`AFFORDANCE-PRESENT`, `AFFORDANCE-ABSENT`,
`NEUTRAL`, and the blind-prediction scoring) are computed against **160/461**, battery split
as receipted: 20 Tier-1 / 107 GSM8K / 33 MBPP (prior 162 = 20/108/34).

Status of the prior number: **162/461 is retained, not superseded as a measurement.** It is
the Stage 2B-S K1 result under the Stage 2B-S execution schedule, tagged to that evaluator,
and it remains the depth study's banked comparator for Paper Two. It is simply not a valid
comparator for cells scored by a different evaluator. Neither number is described as a
regression of the other — they are the same frozen model read by two instruments whose
disagreement (net −2) is inside the instruments' documented resolution.

Grounds:

- **Identity gate passed exactly.** Scored-logit max abs diff 0.0; committed-cache max abs
  diff 0.0 at α=0. The evaluator adds nothing at α=0; the 160-vs-162 difference is therefore
  attributable entirely to execution schedule, not to the recirculation graph.
- **Churn magnitude and signature match R-1 instrument physics.** 28 flipped rows against a
  previously measured ~40-label cross-runtime churn; net −2 inside the ±6–9 floor; flips
  GSM8K-dominated with Tier-1 stable at 20/20 — exactly the margin-concentrated,
  long-generation cascade signature we registered when we characterized BF16 schedule churn.
- **No-hybrid-cells law.** Evaluator identity (schedule, batching, precision, reader, cache
  ownership, serving graph) is part of the measurement. A comparator measured on evaluator X
  may not anchor cells measured on evaluator Y. The stop was therefore mandatory and correct.
