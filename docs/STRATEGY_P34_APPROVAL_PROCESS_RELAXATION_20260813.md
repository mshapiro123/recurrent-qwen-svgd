## 3. The non-monotonicity clause, standing for all curve readings

Per Mark: science is not a straight line, and the program's readings must not assume it is. Double descent, grokking, and damage-then-recovery transients are real dynamics on record in this field and in this program's own history. Therefore:

- **A plateau or dip inside one wave is a prior, not a verdict.** Curve classifications like the i1 `capacity_next_tail_plateau` steer the next lever choice but never close a door — duration extension remains a standing cheap lever alongside whichever lever the classification recommends, exercisable at any report-back under the wave rule.
- **Demotions and warnings tolerate transients by design.** Rungs are re-earned, Tier-W is review-then-continue, and a transient decline that precedes improvement is exactly what the reversible tiers exist to ride through. No exploration reading terminates a lever, an arm, or a phase on a single wave's shape.
- **Only the cliffs are verdicts** — non-finite training, lineage corruption, sealed-data contact, and the calibrated catastrophic stop. Everything else is a curve still being drawn.
