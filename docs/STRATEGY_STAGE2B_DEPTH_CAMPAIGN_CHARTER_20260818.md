## 1. The rationale — read this first

The evidence chain that produced this campaign, in order. **(1)** The K+ sweep and T1 geometry showed the loops *run but idle*: state moves smoothly toward K=4 while task output stays flat, because the depth-lottery objective is indifferent between using depth and ignoring it — depth-invariance is a trained optimum, not a failure. **(2)** The looped-LM literature (Huginn/recurrent-depth, Ouro, successors) shows real reasoning gains from iterated latent computation, but in *every* positive result the looped block itself is trained with loop-aware objectives at large token budgets; no one has shown gains from steering a fully frozen block through a sidecar — that gap is simultaneously this campaign's risk and its novelty. **(3)** The channel audit found our one zero-parameter channel is in-loop compute: the sidecar steers, but nothing inside the loop can adapt. The old "capacity was never binding" verdict (i1) was measured under the depth-indifferent objective and does not transfer. **(4)** The measurement-floor result (±6–9 rows from runtime numerics alone) means only ≥floor effects are worth training for, and continuous margins are the only sub-floor-sensitive steering signal. **(5)** Mark's directive: the regimen must be a curated curriculum with per-element learning rates; the dose must be materially higher than prior campaigns; and the campaign must carry a cheap, preregistered escape if the data says the hypothesis is wrong. The design below exists to satisfy exactly these five facts. The staged-investment logic: this campaign costs 2–4× a standard run and either delivers the program's central thesis (a frozen small model made to genuinely reason deeper through trained attachments — claimable on the primary batteries) or banks the boundary result that settles Paper Three's biggest architecture question (loops must be built into midtraining, not retrofitted) before the 100–1000× spend.

## 2. Architecture (composes only validated or adopted components)

- **Substrate:** frozen Qwen2.5-0.5B, unchanged; T=1/base path bit-exact always (see LoRA gating below).
- **Sidecar:** the validated Phase 3 system, initialized from the P3.5 Arm-S EMA endpoints (seed 0 `a047e2e7…26ca6`, seed 1 `e36cddb7…7fccb`).
- **mHC scratchpad** per the adoption memo, PR-1 resolved to the default: **lanes × slots, S ∈ R^{4×8×128}**, Birkhoff routing on the lane axis only (log-space Sinkhorn, 20 iters, ε_r/ε_c logged), identity-favoring init, ρ-interpolated mixing.
- **Constitutive innovation** (Full-bandwidth): Δ^R = W_H h_k ⊙ σ(W_P p) — the recurrent path cannot be bypassed.
- **Loop-scoped LoRA (the (c) absorption and the capacity repair):** rank-16 adapters on the recurrent block's attention projections (~1.4M params), zero-init B per house style, and **loop-conditioned scaling: contribution exactly zero on the first pass, active only for loops k ≥ 2.** This preserves the base-path bit-exact guarantee permanently while giving the loops trainable compute. Unit test required: base-path logits identical with and without adapters installed, at init and at every checkpoint.
- **Excluded by design:** memory bank (suspended per CV-1), draft head inactive for scoring (standing), no cross-token persistence (mHC carry lives *within* a token's K loops; the fresh-scratchpad-per-token inference contract stands).

## 3. Objective — deeper strictly better

Primary loss: **full-sequence forward KL to the pinned 14B teacher** (not answer-masked — this is the dose lever: ~20–50× more supervised signal per token than the answer-masked campaigns), computed per loop, plus CE on teacher tokens, with two depth-progressive mechanisms:

1. **Monotonicity hinge:** L_mono = λ_m · Σ_k relu(KL_k − KL_{k−1} + δ) — every loop is penalized for failing to improve on its predecessor by margin δ. This is the anti-idle term; the depth lottery is retired per the randomize/order doctrine (fixed K = 4, deep supervision on all loops).
2. **Execution-verified depth targets (GSM8K/MBPP arm):** where k-step partial solutions can be constructed and programmatically verified, loop k's auxiliary target is the verified k-step state. You design the depth-target generator; the principle bound here is that these targets are execution-checked, never teacher-sampled, so teacher ignorance cannot cap them. If generator construction proves heavier than priced, the monotonicity hinge alone is the fallback and the arm is dropped with disclosure.

Exact loss weights, δ, λ_m, and the lattice/top-K details are yours to bind in the executed lock, with the standing per-loss floor discipline if more than two loss components are live.

## 4. Regimen — the curated curriculum with per-element learning rates

M-stages adapted from the adoption memo (M5/M6 omitted with memory suspended), each with a verification gate before the next opens: **M0** exact-baseline reproduction (bit-exact identity receipt); **M1** replicated lanes, P = I, lane-1 read — must reproduce the single-lane system; **M2** train reads/writes/innovation, P = I, LoRA still zero; **M3** unlock ρ-interpolated mixing and ramp loop-LoRA; **M4** full dynamic routing, full objective. ## 7. The kill-gate — Mark's wrong-path protection, preregistered

**Gate position: after M3, at ~20 percent of budget (≈ step 5,000).** Gate statistic: per-loop margin separation on held-out DEV-2 margins — mean margin of loop k strictly above loop k−1, with a bootstrap interval excluding zero for at least the K=3→4 and K=2→3 transitions. Rule: **at least one seed separating → continue to M4 and the full budget; both seeds flat → terminate.** On termination: the boundary result "adapter-scale loop-aware training does not induce depth use through a frozen substrate at ~10⁹-token dose" is banked as a registered finding, the remaining budget rolls to Paper Three preparation, and no endpoint is scored as a capability claim. You bind the exact margin estimator and interval procedure in the lock; the position and the rule are fixed here. This gate is the reason the campaign is safe to run without the certainty no experiment can provide: the decisive signal is purchased at the cheapest point of the spend.
