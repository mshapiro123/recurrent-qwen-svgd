## 1. The classification is ratified

Nothing exploded — the *distribution moved and the ruler didn't*. The clip ceiling was empirically grounded — but **grounding it at step zero was a mistake, and a foreseeable one (Mark, 2026-08-06)**, not merely a reference that later went stale. Step-zero gradient statistics are the *least* representative sample of the trajectory a constraint will police: the model sits at its init transient, every loss is at its maximum, and the distribution's subsequent drift was not hypothetical — A1's own receipts showed gradient shares moving substantially away from their step-0 calibration values, the same phenomenon, already measured, in the immediately preceding stage. Measuring *at all* satisfied the letter of measure-then-constrain while sampling exactly the wrong population. The corollary is adopted as standing, replacing the weaker phrasing: **calibration snapshots ground initializations only; any constraint that persists through training must be referenced to the population it will actually police — trajectory statistics (rolling windows) or nothing. A step-zero snapshot is never that population, and static thresholds on evolving statistics are stale by construction, not by bad luck.** The audit's own caveat is also honored: the small negative immediate effects mean this supports *continuation*, not a positive mechanism verdict — no reading of the step-237 numbers changes any endpoint bar.

## 5. Plain-language summary

The run tripped another of our own alarms — this one a limit on gradient size that was calibrated on day one and never updated, while healthy training naturally shifted the numbers a few percent past it. Mark's review named the sharper truth: calibrating that limit on day one was the mistake itself, not just a limit that aged — day-one numbers are taken at the single strangest moment of training, and the previous phase had already shown us, in its own receipts, how far the numbers drift from there. The forensics are unambiguous: the training signal at the stop was almost entirely the intended objective, pointing the right way, producing a tiny, finite update on an ordinary batch. So: resume from the exact saved states, feed the very batch that tripped the alarm (skipping it would quietly bias the data), replace the fixed limit with one that travels with the training and only fires on a true runaway, and — the coding agent's sharpest catch — rewind the data-shuffler state precisely, because one seed's shuffler had silently moved past the rejected batch. The four true cliffs (corrupted model, broken science, garbage numbers, real quality damage) keep their hair-trigger stops; everything else measures and reports.
