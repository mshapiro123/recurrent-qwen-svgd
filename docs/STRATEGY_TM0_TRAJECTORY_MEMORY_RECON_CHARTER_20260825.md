## Plain-language summary

The question behind this line is whether a small model can be handed, at the moment it begins its private computation, a memory of *where computation productively went* the last time it stood somewhere similar — direction of successful movement through representation space, not remembered content. Our program has already killed content-valued memory (CV-1) and fixed correction directions (W1), and it has one live positive: conditional structure in the correction field. TM-0 spends almost nothing — two teacher caching passes and a stack of closed-form desk mathematics — to answer the two questions everything downstream depends on: can teacher geometry be affinely stitched into the student's frame well enough to matter (we have one strong precedent that it can), and do the displacement fields of successful teacher computation have low-dimensional, stratum-consistent, discriminative structure when expressed in the student's coordinates? If either answer is no, the trajectory-memory line stops before a single memory table is built. If both are yes, and the W2′ desk gates are also green, the next charter builds the frozen nearest-neighbor memory and conditions the student on it — still forward-only, still at 0.5B.

## 1. The hypothesis under test

**H-TM:** P(d_productive | h, Δh) — the distribution over productive future directions of computation, conditioned on current state and current direction of travel — contains reusable information across problems, expressible in the 0.5B student's coordinate frame.

This is the nonparametric sibling of the W2′ estimand (the conditional mixer estimates the same conditional expectation parametrically). The two waves inform each other by design: W2′'s D1/D2 gates test conditional structure in the *student's own* correction field; TM-0 tests whether *teacher* computation supplies richer versions of the same directions. The joint decision table is in §7.

**1a. What the banked L0b failure does and does not rule out (scope clause).** W1's L0b — 14B late-layer Procrustes state-deltas injected as fixed-γ perturbations at the *late* write interface — failed outright (−0.1843, ≈ its own shuffle; my Stage-0 registered prediction that L0b would lose was right). That result rules out *that estimator at that locus*: glued teacher states as direct late injections. It does not test TM's claim, which differs on all three axes L0b held fixed: displacement *fields* rather than states, *retrieval over success strata* rather than own-row targets, and *early conditioning* rather than late injection (the row-13 depth gradient — retrieval quality falls layer 6 → 24 → loop pool — is the standing evidence that early is where cross-model geometry is legible). L0b is therefore the registered null hypothesis of this line, not its refutation; TM-1′'s controls are what would separate the outcomes.

## 2. Objects and definitions

- **Student boundary state h₀** — the 0.5B state at the prelude→middle interface (output of layer 5), the callosum interface of the bicameral graph; per T1 this is the base model's own state at all-gates-zero.
- **Prelude velocity δh_P** — h^(5) − h^(5−r), r = 2 registered (the direction the prelude was taking). The TM query object is [h₀; δh_P], matching the R-G state+velocity feature law and W2′'s FS-2.
- **Teacher states** — cached per-layer states of Qwen2.5-7B and Qwen2.5-14B on the registered panels (§3), at the same pinned runtime, BF16, sequential execution, declared execution schedule (the batch-shape law applies to teachers too until shown otherwise — assert per-teacher T1-style batch invariance on 8 probe rows before caching at batch >1, or cache sequentially).
- **Stitches A_j** — split-fit ridge maps from teacher layer j (d=3584 or 5120) to the student boundary frame (d=896): A* = (H_Tᵀ H_T + λI)⁻¹ H_Tᵀ H_S, cross-fitted (fit/eval halves, both directions), λ from a pre-registered log-grid. **Gauge law (binding):** all stitches used in any displacement computation are fit against the *same* student reference frame (h₀), so teacher displacements at different depths live in one coordinate system; no mixed-frame subtraction.
- **Teacher displacement** — d_i = A_{j+Δ} h_i^(j+Δ) − A_j h_i^(j), with direction u_i = d_i/‖d_i‖ and magnitude a_i = ‖d_i‖. Δ registered per teacher as ~25% of remaining depth past j* (exact values fixed by the agent in the pre-run receipt, before any scoring).
- **Capability strata**, anchored to the student: **D_{7>0.5}** (7B correct, 0.5B wrong), **D_{14>0.5}**, **D_{14>7}** (the transition rung), **D_all** (all correct), **D_none** (all wrong — the control stratum his note omits and we add: structure present only in success strata is the interesting outcome; structure equally present in D_none is generic depth-flow).

## 4. TM-1 — Interface recon (the stop-rule experiment)

For each teacher: compute the debiased-CKA layer-alignment matrix between h₀ and every teacher layer; identify j* = argmax; fit cross-fitted ridge stitches at j* and neighbors (j*±2); evaluate held-out reconstruction of h₀ against two controls — mean-state (predict the student mean) and random-projection (norm-matched) — on cosine and relative MSE.

**Identifiability note (C6 lesson, applied):** a ridge stitch predicts the sample mean in directions the fit cannot identify, so the mean-state control is partially nested inside the stitch — the stitch-vs-mean-state comparison is therefore computed in the **whitened** metric, where unidentified directions contribute zero advantage by construction, and the raw-metric version is reported alongside for continuity.

**GATE G-TM1 (per teacher):** the stitch's held-out advantage over both controls clears a pre-registered margin (relative MSE reduction ≥ 20% vs mean-state; cosine gap ≥ 0.10 vs random-projection), both fit halves. **If G-TM1 fails for both teachers, TM stops here** — key `STITCH-DEAD`, the cross-model latent memory line is unattractive at this scale, and the receipts say so before any memory exists. Whitening covariance for all TM mathematics is estimated with RMT shrinkage on the *broad* pool (all cached rows, all strata) — never on a single stratum. (Mark's broad-corpus-for-geometry point, folded in at the scale we can afford; a Wikipedia-slice geometry pool is a registered option for TM-1b if the panel covariance proves rank-starved.)

## 5. TM-2 — The successful-computation-direction survey

On G-TM1 pass: compute displacement fields per teacher per stratum; SVD each stacked D-matrix; report variance-explained profiles at r ∈ {8, 32, 128}; principal-angle overlap between strata (D_{7>0.5} vs D_{14>7} vs D_all vs D_none); and the two discriminative reads, cross-fitted: (i) do top displacement components separate success-stratum rows from D_none rows? (ii) **the bridge read:** principal-angle overlap between the success-stratum displacement subspaces and the principal subspace of the student's own oracle correction field (c\*_a, banked from W1/W3) — with the common mode removed from both, per the D1 lesson. This one comparison connects the two programs: it asks whether the "extra computation" of a stronger teacher points where our injectable corrections point.

**Battery-stratification law (the KP-1 lesson, applied before it can bite).** The capability strata are strongly confounded with battery — D_{7>0.5} will be GSM8K-heavy, the banked clusters *are* task families, and KP-1's original sin was a discriminative probe whose target covaried with benchmark identity (the target-entropy audit is now standing doctrine). Therefore: **every stratum contrast in TM-2 — SVD comparisons, principal-angle overlaps, and both discriminative reads — is computed within-battery first and pooled second**, with per-battery tables in the receipts; a contrast that exists only across batteries is reported as `BATTERY-CONFOUNDED`, never as success-direction structure. A target-entropy audit (does the discriminative label actually vary within each battery-stratum cell?) precedes any discriminative read.

**Registered outcome keys:** `DIRECTIONS-STRUCTURED` (low-rank structure present, success-strata mutually consistent above D_none baseline, discriminative both fit halves, surviving within-battery stratification) / `DIRECTIONS-DIFFUSE` (no such structure — H-TM weakened at this scale) / `DIRECTIONS-GENERIC` (structure present but equally in D_none — depth-flow, not success information) / `BATTERY-CONFOUNDED` (structure is task-family identity in disguise) / `STRATUM-SPLIT` (strata disagree — return to strategy, no branch).
