Larger effects come from more capture (capacity, curriculum, persistence — the P3.5 levers), not from more steps alone. Expectations for the amended run are set accordingly.

## 2. What the campaign established

**Finding one: token-level correction converts to task-level answers.** This was the question P3.4 existed to ask, and the answer is yes — small, replicated, directionally consistent across two independent seeds and the slot arm, at every look. The conversion question that has hung over the program since the charter is now answered in the affirmative at exploration grade.

**Finding two: the controller's recovery action is measured to be counterproductive.** Rung demotion changes the forward and loss geometry; it does not act on gradient shares. In two of three arms it made the dominant loss more dominant. This is a clean, well-instrumented negative result about a design coupling — annealing rungs and objective health are different control problems and the lock wired one to the other. The agent's diagnosis in §5.4 is adopted in full.

**Finding three: an estimator boundary between audit collateral and task harm.** χ measured 0 on the registered audit slices while GSM8K lost rows in all three arms and Tier-1 lost the same two rows in every endpoint. No contradiction — the audit slices are drawn from the agreement stratum and the task panel is a different population — but it means the collateral estimator does not bound task-level regression, and no one should read χ = 0 as "no harm anywhere." This joins the e2 lesson (guardrails bind at the estimator they are calibrated on) as a standing scope note, and it is precisely what the stratified diagnostic in §4 below is for.

## 3. The six questions, ruled

**Q1 — continuation or restart.** Amended continuation is approved in principle, with one restriction: no arm resumes from a checkpoint trained under a degenerate allocation. Seed 0's final 300 steps ran with aim near 76 percent and KL near 12 — those states carry the pathology the amendment exists to fix. The rule, to be bound per arm in a2: **resume from the latest evaluation checkpoint whose trailing share window satisfied every floor** — the last-healthy checkpoint — with its hash pinned in the amendment. The coding agent computes these from the cached windows. Where the last-healthy point is very early (the slot arm's would be near initialization), continuation offers nothing over restart, which is one more reason for the Q5 ruling below.

**Q2 — decouple share recovery from rung demotion.** Yes, ruled affirmatively and adopted as the a2 design principle. Objective-share recovery becomes a weight-space action: per-loss multiplicative log-weight feedback toward target shares, clipped per update, applied only at 100-step window boundaries, with the counterfactual expected shares recorded before each application — the handoff's recommended candidate and creative alternative two, merged. The annealing rung is reserved for what it was chartered for: capability scheduling on task and causal evidence. Rung demotion remains the Tier-W consequence; it stops being the share-repair mechanism. This is the two-timescale structure of creative alternative four, and it is the right one.

**Q3 — rung-specific share targets.** Yes, conditional on the autopsy. The measured allocation shift after demotion is direct evidence that a single share-target vector across rungs mis-specifies the contract. The CPU-only controller autopsy (handoff recommended step 2) is **authorized now** — it is desk work under decide-document-proceed authority, no ratification needed. Its deliverable: per-rung reconstructed share distributions and the static weight vector per rung that would have satisfied the floors, plus a statement of whether any static vector suffices or whether the dynamic controller of Q2 is necessary. The autopsy's numbers become a2's calibration bindings. If cached gradients show the floors are algebraically unattainable at some rung — genuine gradient conflict rather than mis-weighting — the bounded PCGrad-style comparison from creative alternative three is pre-approved as an additional autopsy arm, on cache only.

**Q4 — the battery pattern.** The stratified diagnostic is approved as DEV-only telemetry, CPU on cached rows, explicitly barred from checkpoint selection. The pattern is worth a mechanism look before more training because it is consistent across three arms, and because the claim structure depends on it: under the reasoning-scope addendum, GSM8K and MBPP are the primary targets and MMLU is floor-only. The honest target-half accounting — +4, +3, +3 rows, closing 1.9 to 2.6 percent of the target-half gap — is thinner than the pooled line, and the pooled gains lean on a floor battery we pre-committed not to claim. The diagnostic should ask three things: whether the gate opens differentially by battery, whether GSM8K regressions co-locate with open-gate writes (which would make them collateral the audit estimator cannot see, per §2 finding three), and what the two lost Tier-1 rows are. Tier-1 is 25 rows of high-confidence floor — the same two rows lost in all three arms is the preservation contract's territory, and with a1's preserve-amplification watch item (74× and 86×) already standing, I want those rows named before the amended campaign runs.

**Q5 — the slot arm.** Shelved, not killed — retained as banked archaeology with its endpoint preserved. The fork's premise was that slot supervision attacks the information side; the arm's own receipts undercut the urgency — the slot objective stayed funded while the system stopped for the same controller reasons as the main arms, and no task advantage over main appeared in the window observed. The caveat is recorded plainly: 400 steps is not a fair test, and this is a shelving on cost grounds, not a falsification. Revisit trigger: if the repaired main campaign completes its budget and plateaus below the effect needed for confirmation, the slot arm returns as a P3.5 candidate alongside capacity and persistence, on equal footing.

**Q6 — the confirmation bar.** A paired-confidence criterion, set by power calculation rather than by a number picked today. The a2 amendment will bind: (a) the CONFIRM panel size and estimator, per the sealed design; (b) the minimum DEV endpoint effect — expressed in rows, points, and gap_closed — such that a one-sided paired test on CONFIRM has at least 80 percent power at α = 0.05, using DEV-measured discordance rates; (c) the eligibility gate: both main seeds positive at the registered endpoint (not best look), pooled target-half delta non-negative in both seeds, per the t1 claim-coherence rule. One honesty note the power calculation must confront: at the currently measured effect size and 1,024-row panel scale, the paired CI half-width is roughly two points against an effect under one point. If CONFIRM at its sealed size cannot reach power at the ceiling-consistent effect, the right responses are pooling seeds as a pre-registered joint test or accepting a larger minimum effect as the bar — not narrowing the CI by optimistic assumptions. Better to learn that arithmetic now than after spending CONFIRM.

## 6. Plain-language summary

The big question this run existed to answer came back yes: the reasoning sidecar makes the small model answer real questions slightly better, and it did so in every run we tried. The improvement is honest but small — six to nine more correct answers out of a thousand — and the statistics can't yet rule out luck, so nobody gets to say "better model" yet. Training stopped early in all three runs because of a thermostat problem in the training controller: when the balance between learning objectives drifted, the controller's fix made the imbalance worse, and the safety rule then shut things down exactly as written. The fix is narrow and cheap to design — repair the thermostat, change nothing else — and the desk analysis to design it is authorized now, using data we already have. Two patterns get a closer look before training resumes: the gains came more from code and general-knowledge questions than from the math questions we most care about, and a couple of easy questions the model used to get right went wrong in every run. The three trained models are kept; the two main ones will resume from their last healthy point once the repair is approved. The slot experiment is paused, not discarded. The sealed final exams stay sealed, and before we ever open them we will do the arithmetic to make sure they can actually settle the question at this effect size. One signature from Mark on the repair amendment restarts training.
