# Strategy — Phase 3 Dialectical Review: The Case Against the Opening Charter, and What It Changes

Date: 2026-08-09. Subject: `STRATEGY_PHASE3_OPENING_HANDOFF_20260809.md` (Drive `1oIHPwaZff6n1YvqwrHTI2EXWTl6TM7n_`, SHA `b46fd2fc…829a8`). Method: thesis (the charter as published), antithesis (the strongest honest case against it), lessons audit (every banked lesson from Paper One and the Phase 2 campaign checked against the plan), synthesis (seven amendments, each assigned to a specific lock). Requested by Mark: "have we addressed all of the lessons learned so far on Paper One and Paper Two? Is the plan clearly able to address the larger aims?"

Verdict up front: the plan's causal chain survives the attack, but the antithesis lands three real blows — one on claim coherence, one on confirmation coherence, one on a loss-set conflict of exactly the species that killed the Phase 2 joint pilot. All three are repairable before any GPU spend, and none requires reordering the sequence. The lessons audit finds two more unnamed thresholds — the precise defect class behind three of our four false stops. Amendments below.

## 1. The antithesis: three attacks

**Attack 1 — Aim may be unlearnable in principle, not just in practice.** The oracle direction d*(p) is the gradient of the margin toward the *teacher's* token y_t. On flip-candidate positions the student, by definition, does not know y_t — that is what makes it a flip candidate. So the charter is asking the bridge to compute, from (h_p, S), a direction whose definition depends on information the student's forward pass got wrong. The rebuttal has teeth but is narrower than the charter implies: the flow state carries 1.36 nats toward the teacher's *future distribution*, which is evidence that partial information about y_t is present — but 1.36 nats is a distributional statement over 8×128 dims, and picking the flip direction for a *specific* token in a 151,936-way vocabulary may demand far more. The charter's answer is P3.3, which is correct, but P3.3 costs an A100 session and the question has a nearly-free preliminary answer: a ridge probe from cached (h_p, S) to cached d* on CPU gives an upper-bound-flavored prior on achievable cosine alignment before any training run. If the probe ceiling is near zero, π near zero stops being a surprise and starts being a prediction — and we will have learned it for pocket change. The falsifier stays; it gains a forecast.

**Attack 2 — The plan swaps one trap for its mirror image.** Phase 2's trap: a 99.5% retention floor that made "smarter" unreachable by construction. Phase 3's floor: −3 points on the reference battery. Between those two numbers is an enormous tolerated region. A run that lands gap_closed = +0.04 on the math battery while sitting 2.5 points below base on general knowledge trips no rule, advances rungs, and reaches confirmation — and the resulting model is *not a better model*. It is a differently-shaped model with a press release. Paper One's own boundary finding predicts this failure mode precisely: *off task, extra loops are not free*. The charter defends against catastrophe but not against Pyrrhic success, and nothing in it makes the paper's headline claim — a better 0.5B — arithmetically honest. The repair is claim discipline, not a new training gate (adding a tight training gate would rebuild the Phase 2 trap): gap_closed is reported *jointly* with the general-battery delta, and the confirmed claim "better model" requires the aggregate accounting to be non-negative. Training explores freely above the cliff. The *claim* pays for what it broke.

**Attack 3 — The confirmation partition does not exist for this currency.** P3.6 says "the sealed evaluation partition is spent exactly once." The sealed asset is EVAL-E — anchors built for acceptance and retention measurement. gap_closed is denominated in *battery* accuracy on public task sets (ARC, math, code). EVAL-E cannot adjudicate a battery claim, and public batteries are not sealed — every checkpoint window of P3.4 reads the DEV battery, and by P3.6 the program will have looked at those distributions hundreds of times. This is the EVAL-D incompatibility discovered at E1, about to be rediscovered at the worst possible moment. The repair costs nothing if done at P3.1 and is impossible to do honestly later: at battery assembly, split every battery into a DEV half and a CONFIRM half, hash and seal the CONFIRM halves before any model — base, teacher, or augmented — is scored on them, and register that P3.6 spends the CONFIRM halves once. EVAL-E stays sealed for what it can actually confirm: acceptance-byproduct and retention claims.

## 2. The lessons audit

Every banked lesson, where the charter addresses it, and the finding. Format: lesson → disposition.

**From Paper One:**

1. *Off task, extra loops are not free* → *partially addressed.* Battery floor catches catastrophe only. Attack 2's tolerated region is exactly this lesson unlearned. → Amendment A1.
2. *Acquisition-retention boundary; recovery rather than coexistence* → addressed in structure (annealed preserve, two-consecutive-evals debounce tolerates damage-then-recovery transients), but retention is listed only as telemetry with no trajectory view. Cheap fix: retention tracked as a trailing-window trend in the standard curves, so recovery-in-progress is visible and distinguishable from decay. → folded into A1's reporting block.
3. *Depth-selection negative; router instability* → **addressed head-on.** The trained gate with oracle open/close labels is that lesson converted into a supervised problem. This is the charter's cleanest inheritance.
4. *Single-seed fragility* → addressed. Both seeds at P3.3, seed-stable requirement before P3.6.
5. *"Magnitude was never the problem; direction was"* → addressed by construction. L_aim is direction-only, magnitude stays governed by clamp and gate.

**From the Phase 2 campaign:**

6. *Every threshold names its estimator in the same clause* (A1 false stop) → **violated twice in the charter.** χ_max appears in the rung table and is never given a value or an estimator. π's estimator (which positions, which pooling across seeds, what CI) is described in prose but not pinned. Three of four false stops began exactly here. → Amendment A2.
7. *Endpoint bars bind at endpoints; in-flight rules take trajectory grounding* (A2 false stop) → addressed and consistent. The battery floor is deliberately in-flight because it guards the fourth cliff, and its reference — the frozen base's score on a fixed battery — is a true constant, so static grounding is correct here (unlike the stale step-zero clip ceiling, whose reference was nonstationary).
8. *False-stop tax* → *not paid.* Nobody computed the probability that the −3-point floor trips under the null. If the reference battery is small (an ARC-Challenge slice can carry ~1.5-point eval noise), two consecutive noisy evals can fire the one cliff we swore was real. The floor's meaning depends on battery size, and the charter never states it. → Amendment A3.
9. *Constraint interference — "did we clip things in another step?"* (the joint-pilot post-mortem) → **one live conflict found.** L_KL pulls the student toward the teacher on *all* supervised positions. On positions where the lattice's verifier says the teacher is *wrong* and the student is right, L_KL actively teaches the student the teacher's mistake while L_CE and preserve pull the other way — two masters, same tokens, the β-ladder-versus-trust-rail pattern in miniature. The lattice already carries verifier labels, so the fix is a mask, not a mechanism. → Amendment A4.
10. *Data quantities only with receipt fields* → addressed. 181,969 carries its provenance. The high-teachability stratum count is honestly deferred to P3.2's report-back rather than invented.
11. *EAGLE data-starvation comparison done up front* → addressed-with-a-note. The sidecar's 1.18M trainable parameters put modest demands on 181,969 anchors, and data expansion is a named P3.5 lever. The lock should still state the tokens-per-parameter arithmetic so the comparison is on paper before the run, not after.
12. *Every run is a curve, not a verdict* (the undertraining/grokking review) → addressed. Standing per-window curves, 20-point pattern carried over.
13. *Oracle staleness under distribution shift* → addressed. Known-limitation clause with a named revisit (direction refresh in P3.5).
14. *Score-blind and sealed-partition discipline; read-once leases* → addressed for EVAL-E, **incoherent for the new currency.** Attack 3. → Amendment A5.
15. *Same reader protocol across scales* (E1's EVAL-D lesson) → addressed. P3.1 scores base and teacher with one reader.
16. *Bounded claim phrasings* (style guide, claim ledger) → mostly addressed via do-not-claim; A1 adds the aggregate-accounting requirement that makes "better model" a bounded, arithmetically-backed phrase.

Two further audit notes below threshold for amendments but worth ink in the locks: deep supervision at every k multiplies coda-forward cost by K, so the P3.4 lock should permit subsampled k per step (sampled k with w_k weighting is standard and unbiased in expectation). And gap_closed's denominator (A_14B − A_base) can be small on easy batteries, making the ratio noisy — the raw delta A_aug − A_base is reported beside the ratio everywhere, and any battery where the denominator's CI touches zero reports delta only.

## 3. Synthesis: the amendments

Each binds a named lock. None reorders the sequence. None adds a training gate.

- **A1 (claim coherence; P3.1 lock and the paper's claim ledger).** The reference battery's general-knowledge component is scored at every checkpoint window and reported as a trailing trend beside gap_closed. The confirmed headline claim requires: gap_closed > 0 with CI excluding zero on at least one target battery, *and* the pooled accounting across all batteries (target + general, weighted by item count) non-negative. Training above the −3 cliff remains unconstrained. The trap is not rebuilt — the claim is made honest.
- **A2 (name the estimators; P3.3 lock).** χ_max receives a calibrated value derived from the measured collateral of *oracle* writes at matched gate ceiling plus a stated margin, with its estimator (positions, window, seed pooling) in the same clause. π's estimator is pinned identically: held-out audit slice, flip rate ratio at matched clamp and gate accounting, both seeds pooled with a bootstrap CI. No threshold enters the lock without its estimator in the same sentence.
- **A3 (pay the false-stop tax; P3.1 lock).** The reference battery is sized so that the probability of the −3-point floor tripping on two consecutive evals under the null (no true change) is below 1 in 10,000, computed from the battery's binomial noise at the base's measured score. If the natural sets are too small, the floor widens or the battery grows — the number is chosen, stated, and defended before the first training step.
- **A4 (verifier mask on distillation; P3.3/P3.4 locks).** L_KL and L_CE are masked (or in the lock's discretion, sharply downweighted) on positions the lattice's verifier marks teacher-wrong. The student is never trained toward a verified teacher error. This removes the identified two-masters conflict at source.
- **A5 (a sealed partition that can adjudicate the claim; P3.1 lock).** At battery assembly, every battery splits into DEV and CONFIRM halves. CONFIRM halves are hashed and sealed before any model is scored on them, under the standard read-once lease. P3.6 spends CONFIRM once. EVAL-E remains sealed and is spent at P3.6 only for the acceptance-byproduct and retention claims it was built to carry.
- **A6 (the free prior on the falsifier; pre-P3.3, CPU-only, may run inside P3.2's session).** A ridge/linear probe from cached (h_p, S) to cached d* on the training stratum, reporting achievable cosine alignment. Its result gates nothing — it converts P3.3 from a blind experiment into a forecast test, and it is the phase's first creativity slot spent well.
- **A7 (report deltas beside ratios; P3.1 lock).** A_aug − A_base accompanies gap_closed everywhere gap_closed appears. Batteries whose teacher-base denominator CI touches zero report delta only.

## 4. Is the plan able to address the larger aim?

The aim is a chain with four links, and the plan now measures every one of them. The state must contain teacher information — measured, 1.36 nats. The channel must reach wrong answers — measured, 60.85% oracle flips and +3.91 to +5.61 points of final-answer ceiling. Trained aim must capture a usable fraction of oracle aim — the open question, P3.3, now with a CPU-cheap prior (A6) and pinned estimators (A2). And captured aim must compound into battery-level improvement that survives honest accounting — P3.4–P3.6, now with a currency that cannot declare victory while quietly losing elsewhere (A1, A7) and a sealed partition that can actually adjudicate it (A5). Attack 1 remains the real scientific risk, and no amendment can argue it away — it is the hypothesis, and the phase is built to test it third, not tenth. What the dialectical pass changed is narrower and matters: before it, a *successful* Phase 3 could have produced an unpublishable claim (no valid sealed partition), a dishonest claim (Pyrrhic success), or a dead pilot from an unnamed threshold — the same three ways Phase 2 actually bled. Now those paths are closed on paper, before the first lock is drafted, which is the only time closing them is free.
